# Example: finished report (medspa, content refresh and merge)

This is the approved shape. Match its structure, length and tone, not its content. In your own report, name the highest-priority page in each theme with its data alongside the theme total.

```markdown
# Balance Aesthetic & Wellness: Content Refresh & Merge Findings & Recommendations

## At a glance

We reviewed your website to identify opportunities to improve visibility in Google and bring more potential patients to your most important service pages. We found the following opportunities to improve your performance:

| Issue | Pages affected |
| --- | --- |
| Pages competing for the same searches | 18 |
| Tampa-area pages that repeat another page | 11 |
| Old redirects that dead-end, hop or point to outdated pages | 73 of 80 reviewed |
| Blog posts with almost no visibility | 38 |
| Auto-generated archive pages | 294 |
| Topics with no page built for them | 3 |

## Biggest opportunities

These are the biggest themes in the audit. The page-by-page list is in the audit spreadsheet.

### 1. Pages competing for the same searches

When two or more of your pages answer the same search, Google has to pick one to show, and none of them gets the full strength of your site behind it. Your pages end up competing with each other instead of with other med spas, so fewer people reach the page that can turn a search into a booking.

- 18 pages should be combined into a stronger page. In each case a blog post or Tampa page appears for many of the same searches as a main service page, and together they earned 8,050 impressions and 1 click

### 2. Tampa-area pages that repeat pages you already have

Like the competing pages above, they split attention between your own pages instead of building it up on one page that can turn a search into a booking.

- 11 pages cover the same treatment or business as another page on your site. Together they earned 7,682 impressions and no clicks.

### 3. Old redirects that don't land on the right page

Old links to your site, from Google and from other websites, can send visitors to an error or a detour instead of the page they wanted, and the search strength those links built up is lost along the way.

- Of 80 existing redirect rules:
    - 46 send visitors to an address that isn't a live page
    - 16 pass through an extra hop
    - 7 point to an outdated destination
    - 4 more point to pages that aren't live.

### 4. Pages with almost nothing going on

These pages give Google more to sort through without bringing in customers, which makes your strongest pages harder to pick out.

- 38 blog posts earned 77 impressions and no clicks between them.
- 294 auto-generated archive pages (gallery, tag, category and similar) earned 236 impressions.

### 5. Searches with no page built for them

People searching for a treatment you offer are being shown a blog article or a device page, not a page that explains the treatment and invites them to book, so those searches are less likely to turn into appointments.

- A blog post on hyperpigmentation laser treatment earned 924 impressions and 1 click for a topic with no service page built around it.
- Two device pages, Alma iPixel and Alma Dye-VL, are mostly found through searches for the treatment rather than the device name.

## Next Steps

These are the actions we will be taking to resolve these issues:

- **Combine competing pages:** merge the useful content from 18 pages into the stronger page for each topic, and redirect the old addresses to it.
- **Review the Tampa-area location pages:** work through the 11 pages that repeat another page and handle each one.
- **Fix the redirects:** remove extra hops, update outdated destinations, and point old addresses to live pages across the 80 rules we reviewed.
- **Retire low-visibility pages:** redirect 38 blog posts to the most relevant service page or blog page, and turn off the 294 auto-generated archive pages so they don't come back.
- **Build for treatment searches:** repurpose the hyperpigmentation blog post as a page about the treatment, and update the Alma iPixel and Alma Dye-VL pages to speak to treatment searches.
```
